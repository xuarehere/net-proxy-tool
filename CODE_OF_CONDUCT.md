# Community Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free environment for everyone participating in the `net-proxy-tool` project, regardless of background, identity, or level of experience.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, discussions, and code reviews.
- Focus on what is best for the community and users.
- Show empathy towards other contributors.
- Gracefully accept constructive feedback.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, derogatory comments, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information of others without permission.
- Disruptive behavior that derails technical discussions.

## Enforcement & Reporting

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the repository maintainers. Project maintainers have the right to edit or remove comments and ban contributors who violate these standards.
